What I'm building

LINDEX

Pre-launch · Founding team of three

A legal case-management platform for people fighting their own disputes and the solicitors who represent them. Two portals, one product: the client organises their own evidence, the firm works the same material as a matter.

I own product and application build. The decision I'd point at first is structural — the client owns the Case, the firm owns the Matter, and an Engagement links the two while the firm is instructed. More objects and more permission logic than a shared record, but when a client changes solicitor they keep their evidence and the firm keeps its file, with no argument about export or deletion.

The build also produced a compliance register of 52 requirements mapped to individual journey steps — including a real conflict between the right to erasure and an immutable audit log, which went to our legal lead as an open ruling rather than getting quietly designed around.
